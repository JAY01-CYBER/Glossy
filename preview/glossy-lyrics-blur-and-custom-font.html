Lyric depth blur & a font of your own

Two asks from the beta group: the lyric stack should read as having depth instead of being one flat column of dimmed text, and the App Font screen should let you bring a typeface with you rather than choosing only from the ones Glossy ships.

1 · Lyrics — the lines away from the singing line blurbefore / after

The singing line stays crisp. Each step behind it is blurred a little more — 1.5dp, 3dp, 5dp, then 7dp — using the same distance the fade already keys off, so the blur and the fade agree about how far a line has receded. Far lines keep their existing dimming on top of the blur.

2 · App Font — import your ownnew

A new row above the list copies a .ttf, .otf or .ttc off the device into the app's own storage and selects it immediately, so the import survives a restart instead of dying with the picker's URI grant. A file that isn't a font is refused before anything is copied, and the picker itself asks for any file at all, because plenty of devices label a .ttf as application/octet-stream and would otherwise grey it out.

Known limit: the blur is a RenderEffect, so it does nothing below Android 12 — Android 10 and 11 keep exactly the fade they have today, and nothing crashes there. Making the depth pass real on older devices would mean blurring bitmaps every frame, which is the wrong trade for animated text.
