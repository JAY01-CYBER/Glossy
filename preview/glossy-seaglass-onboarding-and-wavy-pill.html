Sea-glass Continue, and a wavy slider around the folded pill

Two changes. The onboarding's primary button joins the sign-in card's sea glass, and the mini player's folded bubble gains the progress slider it lost when it gave up its length.

1 — The next Continue button is sea glass

The sign-in card's Google / guest pills are sea glass; the step after them — the guest nickname step and the community page, which end in Start listening and Next — was still a flat violet fill. Both now draw through the same SeaGlassPill, tinted with the onboarding's own violet carried inside the glass.

Disabled really is disabled: SeaGlassPill gained an enabled flag, so the touch is refused rather than accepted and dropped.

One judgement to check: the sign-in pills are pale glass with dark ink, deliberately. This pill is the flow's strongest colour with white ink, so I kept it denser (tintDensity 0.62, transparency 0.45) — it has to stay readable before it has to look glassy. If you'd rather it match the pale pills, say so and I'll flip the tint and ink together.

2 — A wavy slider around the folded mini player pill

Folded, the mini player is just the artwork bubble — and a bar with no length left has nothing to draw its progress hairline under. So the progress wraps it instead: a CircularWavyProgressIndicator traced around the bubble, sized to sit just outside the 48dp artwork without reaching the pill's 64dp edge.
