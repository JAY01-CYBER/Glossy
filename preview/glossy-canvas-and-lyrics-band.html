Left: the phone as it behaves now. Right: after the change. Both are drawn from the two screenshots you sent, at their own geometry, so the difference is the fix and nothing else.

1 · The lyrics band — the opening lines start at the top

The band is not a padding that was added: it is LYRICS_ANCHOR_RATIO. Every line is drawn at anchorY + its offset from the active line, so while the song's first lines are the ones on screen there is nothing above the active line to fill the band. The block is now clamped to the top until there is, and the clamp releases by itself afterwards.

2 · The Spotlight canvas — why a login “fixed” it

The carousel was not refusing to animate. It had been told not to bother, and only a Spotify connect wiped the note. Both flows below start at the same moment: a card on screen, Spotify not connected.

Before — the answer decided while a provider was unasked

1ALL-style race fires: Spotify, ArchiveTune, Tidal, Apple Music.
2Spotify has no credentials → FAILED … and that verdict was counted as an answer.
3The other three reply EMPTY.
4markDefinitiveMiss(mediaId) — “this song has no canvas”, for 15 minutes.
5Every surface asks wasDefinitiveMiss before it looks → the card stops on its still artwork.
6Nothing re-asks. Only invalidateCredentials(), called on a Spotify connect, clears the memory — so the canvas “only loads after login”.

After — an incomplete lookup is not a fact about the song

1A miss is written only when every provider really answered — no FAILED at all.
2Spotify unaskable → no miss → the song stays askable.
3The lookup retries while the surface is on screen: 4 attempts, 2s → 4s → 8s.
4A genuinely canvas-less song still stops on the first attempt: the definitive-miss path is unchanged for it.
5ON_START starts a fresh attempt budget, so a card that failed on a cold radio or before the account was connected recovers by itself.
6Connecting Spotify still clears everything (invalidateCredentials()) — it is simply no longer the only way out.
