What moved across

The reel
The four cover panes now share one scrim, so the 2×2 reads as a single frame rather than four tiles. Back, search and overflow stay floating on top, and the pencil still sits on the seam.
Playing next
The one thing your screen could not answer: a short shelf of what follows the current track, sitting between the controls and the list. It scrolls sideways and never takes over the list.
Ringed mini player
The mini player takes the progress ring from Echo Reel, so the pill is a transport as well as a label. Same three buttons — profile, add, like — in the same order.
Left untouched
Play pill with shuffle and download, the custom-order chip and lock, the artist line with provider icon and duration, and the per-row heart plus overflow. KALYANI stays the playing row, now tinted mint.
