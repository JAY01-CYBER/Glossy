Materialistic mini player · sea glass everywhere · the lyrics fix · the folding pill

Four changes, one page. Everything below is the app's own look reproduced in CSS so you can see the surface and the motion before it ships — the real screens draw it in Compose.

Old devices are the point here. The sea-glass pane is a RuntimeShader pass, which only exists from API 33. Below that it used to fall back to a flat fill — which is why nobody ever saw any glass. The fallback is now a painted pane: the same tint, highlight and chalky rim, drawn directly. Use the device switch below to compare.

1 — The new mini player, made materialistic

A new entry in Appearance → Mini player → Background style: Materialistic (tonal). It is the Material 3 card the rest of the skin is built from — a surfaceContainer body lifted to surfaceContainerHigh behind the artwork, an outlineVariant hairline, and the progress hairline in the primary role, so it repaints from the seed colour and follows Material You.

What actually changed

MiniPlayer.kt · PreferenceKeys.kt · AppearanceSettings.kt

  • MATERIALISTIC joins MiniPlayerBackgroundStyle.
  • Its body is a tonal gradient, not a flat fill, and it keeps the scheme's on-surface ink instead of forcing white.
  • It is painted by both the shared background helper and the new mini player.
  • The style list in Appearance gained Materialistic (tonal).

2 — Slide down and the mini player becomes a round pill

The Apple Music bar already did this. It now happens on every style: scroll a list away from its top and the bar keeps its height, gives up its width from the far end and rounds off, leaving the album artwork as a round bubble. Scroll back to the top and it springs open again. Pick a style and scroll each card.

How it works: the app answers "is the page scrolled off the top?" once at the root and publishes it through LocalAppScrolledToTop. One shared rememberMiniPlayerFold() now feeds one shared container in MiniPlayer(), so every style folds through the same spring. Apple Music keeps its own fold — its artwork pins to the end and its text fades instead of clipping, which the shared wrapper cannot express.

3 — Sea glass in Home, Library and Mix — visible on old devices

Section lockups are now seated on a pane of sea glass on the home feed, in the library and on the mix tab. On API 33 and up it is the real frosted pass; below that the pane is painted. Flip the switch and look closely at the rim and the body's falloff.

In code: a new SeaGlassPanel / SeaGlassSectionHeader owns the surface; SeaGlassSupport.kt now gates on API 33 and exposes Modifier.seaGlassFallback(), the painted pane that older devices get. The pill on the detail hero and the login card pick this fallback up too, so nothing is a flat fill any more.

4 — The lyrics no longer lose their top third

Every lyric line is drawn at anchorY + its offset from the active line, so the anchor ratio is the empty band above the first line when a song starts. At 0.35 a third of the view was blank backdrop before any text appeared. It is now 0.12: the active line starts near the top, with room for the lines above to scroll in.
