Glossy · the screens, after the design

Home, Mix, Library, Search, Settings and the Account sheet as they are drawn now. Home keeps its classic feed — the mood chips, the Featured Spotlight carousel and the new-release row — under the design's greeting card. The geometry, the type scale and the spacing follow the Figma frames, and so do the colours: #101114 page, #1B1D22 surfaces, #24262C mini player, #34353C borders, #F6F2F3 / #A1A0AA text, #EDB8C3 accent. Settings › Appearance › Design palette switches the same layout to the Materialistic skin, where every one of those roles is derived from your seed colour instead. The mini player and the floating pill navigation are the design's own, drawn once and shared by every screen.
