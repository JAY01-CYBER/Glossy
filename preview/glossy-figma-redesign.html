Glossy · the screens, after the design

Home, Mix, Library, Search and Settings as they are drawn now. The geometry, the type scale and the spacing follow the Figma frames; every colour is a Material 3 role — surface, surfaceContainer, primary — so the palette still comes from the theme seed (here the app's own pink) and the mini player is the one you already ship.
