Glossy — Sea Glass surfaces

The sea-glass action pill, drawn once and shared. Colours and material fields are read from the app source, so this is the design the app renders — not an approximation of the idea.

Rendering note. These surfaces are SeaGlass Tier 1 (AGSL RuntimeShader), which requires Android 13 (API 33). Below that the pill paints its flat fill colour instead — same geometry, same ink, no frost. On Android 10 you will see the flat version, which is why this preview exists.
