Lyrics back to the original view, tiles in Search, and Matrix under Chikku

Three edits, drawn from your screenshots at their own geometry: the phone on the left of each pair is what the app did, the phone on the right is what it does now. No APK was built — this page is the preview.

1 · The lyrics view — the original full-screen view draws again

Two views were reachable through one switch: ExperimentalLyrics (the newer one, with the faint provider label and the empty band at the top) and OriginalLyrics. The switch defaulted on, so every install that never opened that settings row got the newer view. It now defaults off — the original view is what the app shows, and the experimental one is one tap away in Appearance exactly as before.

2 · Search rows are tiles — every other list keeps the ghost row

Every row in the app is a ghost row: it paints nothing at rest and only tints while it is playing or selected. Search now asks for tiles instead, so its results carry a resting fill under the artwork and title, the way your screenshot shows. The rule lives in one place (TiledListRows) and is provided by the search screens only — local-library search and online results both, including the suggestion overlay opened on top of a result. No other list asks for it, so nothing else changes.

3 · Matrix moves to the foot of Chikku's card

Matrix (M4TRX) sat in a “Special Helpers” group of their own, far below the two lead developer cards. That credit is now the last block inside Chikku's card — a “Supported by” line with their avatar, name and helper role, tappable through to their GitHub profile. With the group empty it no longer draws at all, so the section and its title are gone until someone new joins it.
