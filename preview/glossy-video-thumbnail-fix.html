Why a video card looked like a letterboxed strip

YouTube Music hands out two different shapes of artwork. Album art is square and the app asks Google's image service to crop it to the tile (…=w544-h544-p-l90-rj), so it fills exactly. A video frame comes from i.ytimg.com as a 16:9 still — 400×225 for hqdefault, 1280×720 for hq720, measured against the live API — and that host ignores the resize suffix entirely (it answers 404), so the app got the raw frame.

The tile is square and the drawing mode was "fit the whole picture", so the frame sat in the middle with two bands of the tile's own background above and below it. That is the circled card: a cover on either side filling its tile, the video between them looking like a smaller picture in a frame.
