2 — The lyrics, first letter of every line

The lyric row applied its 24dp inset before blur() and graphicsLayer instead of after them. Both draw through a layer and clip to its bounds — so the padding shrank those bounds down to the text box, and the row's own rounded clip then took the left edge off the first glyph. The inset now sits inside the blur.

Every line's first glyph is shaved at the same x — the text box edge — so what survives is a two-pixel sliver of the stem.
