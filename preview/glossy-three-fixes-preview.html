1Spotlight canvas not loading

Featured / Spotlight cards stayed still even with Canvas Background on.

The Spotlight canvas shares the same pipeline as the player canvas — CanvasResolver, CanvasVerifier, CanvasPrefetcher — and that pipeline had five bugs (Spotify 429 / missing Client-Token, HEAD probe writing UNPLAYABLE for 30 min on good clips, providers swallowing exceptions into permanent session misses, prefetch using a different artist string than the screen, and the prefetcher silently dropping clips). All five are fixed, so Spotlight now resolves, probes, and plays exactly like the player canvas.

2Mini-player animation fires on every seek, not just on track change

Skipping forward/back 10 seconds caused the song-title row in the Special Edition mini-player to slide out and back in even though the track had not changed.

AnimatedContent(targetState = mediaMetadata) used the full object reference. A seek causes the playback state flow to re-emit the metadata for the same song as a new object, which is not === to the previous one — so Compose saw a state change and fired the slide-in/slide-out transition. The fix keys the transition on mediaMetadata?.id, a String? that is structurally equal across re-emissions of the same track.

3Vinyl player — excessive space above the disc

The spinning vinyl record sat dead-centre in its slot, leaving a large blank band above it between the top bar and the disc.

The artwork zone used weight(1f) with contentAlignment = Alignment.Center, which split leftover height evenly above and below the disc. On a tall phone the gap above was wider than the disc itself. The fix switches to Alignment.TopCenter with padding(top = 8.dp) so the disc sits just below the top bar with a small breathing gap, and all the spare height falls below it (where the transport controls live). The BoxWithConstraints inside uses fillMaxWidth; because its height is now unconstrained, maxHeight is infinite and the existing fittedSide = maxWidth guard sizes the disc correctly by screen width.
