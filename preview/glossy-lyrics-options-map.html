This is the part worth fixing. Six groups stand between the top of the Content screen and the lyrics options, and there is no clue that they are down there — which is why a dedicated Lyrics entry higher up would be the real cure, rather than a fourth reminder of where to scroll.

2 · Spotify lyrics, inside “Provider selection”

This is the older of the two places Spotify appears. It is the fourth switch in the list, under NetEase, Megalobiz and Musixmatch — so if you open this dialog and do not scroll, you may stop before reaching it. This is where it lived before it got its own row.
