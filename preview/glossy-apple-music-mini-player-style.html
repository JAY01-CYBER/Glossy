What changed in the code

One new style, one new signal, nothing else touched.

  • A new MiniPlayerStyle.APPLE_MUSIC, selectable in Appearance → Mini player and shown as “Apple Music (from the video)”.
  • A new AppScrollState + LocalAppScrolledToTop, collected once at the app root from the nested scroll every list already reports through — no screen had to be taught anything.
  • The squiggle is Material 3's LinearWavyProgressIndicator: played part waves, remainder stays flat.
  • The transport capsule is white with dark glyphs, and the skip glyph is the Apple Music one Glossy already ships.
  • Untouched: the Special Edition pill, the Modern and Legacy bars, and the navigation bar.

When nothing provides the signal (a preview, a test), it defaults to expanded, so the bar never folds around a signal it never received.

Same round-collapse idea the tabs already had

There was already a half-built version of this in the code — a sheet of glass that shrinks to a single 48 dp round button on scroll, keyed on an isScrolledToTop flag with a default of true that no screen ever passed, in a bottom bar that is no longer called from anywhere. That is why the effect never appeared on a device. The signal now exists, at the root, and the new style is what reads it.

Not yet seen on a device. The pill and the fold are previewed here and type-checked by the Kotlin compiler; nothing has run on a phone. Two things want one look on hardware: the fold's spring stiffness, and whether a 24 pixel scroll threshold feels right under a real finger.
