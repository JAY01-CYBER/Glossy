Meet Glossy

A beautiful, open-source Android music player built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Material 3 Expressive Design.

Material 3 Expressive

Dynamic colors, squircle shapes, and premium glassy shadows optimized for Android.

Synchronized Lyrics

Powered by LRCLIB API. Read and sing along with perfectly synced lyrics in real-time.

Scrobbling Support

Seamlessly integrated with ListenBrainz to automatically track and scrobble your listening history.

Multi-Language

Fully localized via Crowdin. Enjoy Glossy in English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marwari, and many more.

Jetpack Compose

Built entirely using modern declarative UI, delivering buttery smooth 60fps animations.

100% Open Source

Powered by GitHub Actions & Woodpecker CI. Transparent, ad-free, and community-driven.
